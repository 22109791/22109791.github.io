About Us

Who We Are

Celtec Games is an independent game studio focused on creating thoughtful, handcrafted experiences. We make games driven by curiosity, creativity, and a deep love for the medium.

We believe games don’t have to be massive to be meaningful. Some of the most memorable experiences come from focused ideas, careful design, and a clear creative voice, and that’s what we aim to deliver with every project.

Our Philosophy

We approach game development as both a craft and a conversation.

Every mechanic, visual, and story beat is built with intention. We value clarity over complexity, mood over noise, and player experience over trends. Being independent gives us the freedom to experiment, take creative risks, and follow ideas wherever they lead, even if that path is uncertain.

At Celtec Games, we make the kinds of games we want to play ourselves.

How We Work

Our process is small, flexible, and hands-on

We prototype early, test often, and iterate constantly. Mistakes are part of the process, and feedback especially from players, plays a huge role in shaping our games. We share our progress openly through devlogs and updates because we believe transparency makes better games.

No crunch culture. No filler. Just steady, thoughtful development.

What We’re Making

We’re currently working on original projects that blend atmosphere, narrative, and gameplay in meaningful ways. Whether it’s a quiet moment of exploration or a tense mechanical challenge, our goal is always the same: create experiences that linger after the screen goes dark.

You can follow our current projects on the Games page or through our development blog.
