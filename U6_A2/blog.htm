Building Our First Playable Prototype

Posted on January 18, 2026 · Devlog

Getting to a playable build is a strange milestone. It doesn’t look good. It barely runs. And yet, it’s one of the most exciting moments in development.

After weeks of planning, prototyping mechanics in isolation, and arguing with ourselves over scope, we finally stitched everything together into something you can actually play. Movement is in. The core interaction works. There’s a small environment to explore. And yes, things break constantly.

But this version answered some important questions.

First, it confirmed that the core idea works. The moment-to-moment gameplay feels engaging in a way that’s hard to measure on paper. Second, it revealed problems we couldn’t see before, pacing issues, unclear feedback, and a few mechanics that sounded great but didn’t feel right in practice.

That’s exactly what this stage is for.

Right now, our focus is on iteration. Tightening controls, clarifying systems, and figuring out what to cut just as much as what to keep. We’re also preparing a small closed playtest to gather early feedback, which we’re both excited and slightly terrified about.

As always, thanks for following along. We’ll share more soon.

— Celtec Games
