Celtec Games

Small team. Big ideas. Handmade worlds.


Celtec Games is an independent game studio focused on creating meaningful, memorable experiences. We build games with care, shaped by creativity, curiosity, and a love for the craft. Whether it’s a quiet story, a bold experiment, or a challenging adventure, every project starts with a simple goal: make something we’d want to play ourselves.


We don’t chase trends. We chase feelings, wonder, tension, joy, and discovery. Every world we create is made with intention, shaped by passion, and shared with players who love games as much as we do.

What Makes Us Different?

Our Approach

  • Player-First Design

    • We build games we genuinely want to play and we listen closely to player feedback.

  • Focused Experiences

    • No filler. Every mechanic, story beat, and visual has a purpose

  • Creative Freedom

    • Being indie means we can take risks, try new ideas, and follow our instincts.

  • Craft Over Scale

    • We care more about how a game feels than how big it is.
